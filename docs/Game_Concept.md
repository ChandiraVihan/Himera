 # K47 Exterior Graybox Goals

The K47 exterior should establish a tense first-person approach space before
detail work begins. The graybox pass is successful when the player can read a
clear route, identify useful cover, and understand where the playable boundary
is without relying on finished art.

## Blockout priorities

- Preserve sightline variation across the approach.
- Leave enough room for the existing movement, crouch, sprint, and lean actions.
- Use cover and negative space to create deliberate moments of exposure.
- Keep landmarks large and distinct enough to guide navigation in bodycam-style
	presentation.
